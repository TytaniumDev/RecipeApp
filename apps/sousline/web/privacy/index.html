Privacy policy

Sousline · Effective 13 November 2026

Sousline is a recipe app for iOS, Android, and the web. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: your recipes are yours, the app works without an account, and we never sell your data or show ads.

Using Sousline without an account

You don't need to sign in. Without an account, your recipes, folders, grocery lists, measuring tools, and settings are stored only on your device. We don't receive any of it.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google or Apple to sync between devices and to share recipes. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. We receive your account id, name, and email address. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive the private relay address instead. We use these only to run your account, sync, and sharing.

Sync through your Google Drive

On the free plan, Sousline can sync through your own Google Drive. The app asks only for access to its own hidden app folder (the drive.appdata permission). It can't see, read, or change any of your other Drive files. Your recipe data and photos stay in your Drive, and we don't keep a copy on our servers.

Sousline cloud sync and sharing (Pro)

If you subscribe to Pro, your library syncs through our servers, which run on Google Cloud (Firebase) in the United States. We store your recipes, folders, grocery lists, experiments, and photos there so they can reach your other devices and the people you share with. People you share a recipe or folder with can see what you shared with them, and your name and email are shown to them so they know who shared it.

Subscriptions

Purchases go through the App Store, Google Play, or Stripe on the web. We use RevenueCat to check which subscription you have. We receive your subscription status and an anonymous purchase id, never your card or bank details.

Nutrition lookups and recipe import

  • Common foods are looked up in a database built into the app, so those lookups don't leave your device.
  • Searches for branded foods are sent to the USDA FoodData Central service (through our server) and to Open Food Facts. They receive the search words or barcode, not who you are.
  • When you import a recipe from a link, the app downloads that web page. On the web version, the download goes through our server because browsers block it otherwise. We don't keep the pages or the links you import.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry so we can fix the bug. Reports contain technical details such as the error, the app version, and the device model and operating system. They don't include your recipes, your name, or your email address.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads, use advertising ids, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • We don't use analytics services.
  • We don't use your recipes to train AI models.

Google user data

Sousline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to sign you in and to sync your Sousline data. We don't transfer it to anyone else except as needed to provide those features, to comply with the law, or with your permission, and no person at Sousline reads it unless you ask us to for support.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • You can delete your account in the app under Settings, then Account. This deletes your account and everything we store on our servers for it. A copy stays on your device. Recipes you shared jointly stay with the other people in the share.
  • Data in your Google Drive app folder is yours to keep or remove. You can remove it from Google Drive's settings under Manage apps.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by email (below). We'll do it within 30 days.

Children

Sousline is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app first.
